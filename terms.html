Terms & Conditions

Last updated: September 2026

These Terms & Conditions govern your visit to SOKI Social Sauna and any booking you make through sokisocialsauna.nl. By making a reservation, buying a membership or entering our premises, you agree to these terms.

1. Who we are

SOKI Social Sauna is operated from Europalaan 2B, 3526 KS Utrecht (KVK 93466307). Questions? hello@sokisocialsauna.nl.

2. House rules

SOKI is a shared, quiet-ish space. To keep it a good place for everyone we ask you to:

  • Respect other guests — no loud phone calls, no unsolicited contact, no intimate behaviour in shared areas.
  • Come sober. Alcohol, drugs and being visibly under the influence are not permitted.
  • Shower before entering the sauna. Wear a towel; nudity in the sauna is optional but always covered on benches.
  • Keep phones on silent and out of the sauna and ice bath rooms.
  • Follow instructions from staff at all times. We reserve the right to refuse entry or ask guests to leave if house rules are broken, without refund.

3. Bookings & cancellation

Sessions must be booked online in advance. Capacity is limited and walk-ins are only possible if space is available on the day.

Cancellation refunds are tiered:

  • More than 48 hours in advance: full refund.
  • Between 24 and 48 hours in advance: 50% refund.
  • Less than 24 hours in advance or no-show: no refund. The full amount remains due.

Refunds are issued via the original payment method within a few business days. Weather conditions (snow, storms, KNMI code orange/red) and personal circumstances that make attendance impossible do not entitle you to a refund outside these windows.

If SOKI cancels a session for any reason, you receive a full refund or a free rebooking of your choice.

4. Payment

Payment is processed at the time of booking via Stripe (iDEAL, credit card, Bancontact, SEPA). We do not store card details. Prices are shown in euros and include applicable Dutch VAT.

5. Memberships

SOKI offers monthly memberships that renew automatically until cancelled:

  • Everyday Member (€49/month): 4 credits per month. Everyday Sauna costs 1 credit; Social, Ambient and Aufguss cost 2 credits each. Unused credits do not roll over.
  • Unlimited (€99/month): unlimited access to all session types.

Cancellation: memberships can be cancelled at any time from your account. Access continues until the end of the paid billing period. No partial refunds on already-paid months.

Pauses: in exceptional circumstances (e.g. temporary closure of the venue) we may pause billing for all active memberships. You will not be charged during a pause.

Price changes: we may adjust membership prices with 30 days' notice by email. Your existing subscription keeps its current price until you resubscribe.

6. Gift cards

Gift cards are valid for one year from the date of purchase and can be redeemed for any session or partially applied to a booking. Gift cards are non-refundable, non-transferable to cash, and cannot be replaced if lost.

7. Health & safety

Sauna and ice bath use carry inherent risks. By booking a session you confirm you have read and understood our health disclaimer and that you use our facilities at your own risk. Do not use the sauna or ice bath if you are pregnant, under the influence of alcohol or drugs, have a serious cardiovascular condition, uncontrolled high or low blood pressure, epilepsy, or a fever — unless your doctor has explicitly approved it.

Guests under 16 are not admitted. Guests aged 16–18 must be accompanied by a parent or guardian.

You are liable for damage you cause to our property or to other guests. Reasonable cleaning or repair costs may be recovered from you.

9. Lost & found

We keep items found on the premises for 14 days. After that, unclaimed items are donated to a local second-hand shop or disposed of. We accept no responsibility for items brought onto the premises.

10. Photography & privacy

Photography and filming of other guests inside the venue is not permitted. SOKI may occasionally photograph or film for promotional purposes; we will always ask for explicit consent before including recognisable individuals in published material.

11. Personal data

We process personal data as described in our Privacy Policy.
