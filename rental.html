Rent SOKI

Private hire

The sauna all to yourselves

Book SOKI privately and you get the entire space to yourselves: the sauna, the ice bath and our kantine. Perfect for team outings, birthdays, bachelor(ette) parties or any occasion that deserves warmth. We are happy to help shape the session — with or without aufguss ritual, music or drinks afterwards.

For whom

What can you celebrate at SOKI?

🤝

Team outings & companies

Slow down together. A sauna session breaks the ice faster than any workshop — great as a team outing, celebration or reset.

🎂

Birthdays & groups of friends

Celebrate warm. Book the space with your friends and make an evening of it: sauna, ice bath and drinks in the kantine.

🎉

Bachelor(ette) parties

An original start (or finish) of the day: sweating, plunging and laughing together — no awkward games required.

🎙️

Workshops, shoots & events

Breathwork, yoga, a podcast recording or a photo shoot: our space lends itself to more than sauna alone. Tell us your idea.

What you get

Included in your rental

  • The entire space, exclusively for your group
  • Sauna (up to 15 people per round) and ice bath
  • Showers, changing room and lockers
  • Kantine with (non-)alcoholic drinks
  • A SOKI host to take care of you
  • Optional: aufguss ritual, DJ or your own music
Good to know

Practical

Group size

Up to 15 people in the sauna at once; larger groups can rotate in rounds.

Duration

Most rentals last 2 to 4 hours — we decide together what fits your plans.

When

Also outside our regular opening hours, for example in the morning or on Monday/Tuesday.

Price

Depends on group size, duration and extras. Tell us your plans and we will send a proposal.
