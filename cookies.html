Cookie policy

Last updated: September 2026

This page explains which cookies and similar technologies SOKI uses, what they do, and how you can change your preferences.

Categories

We group cookies into three categories:

  • Necessary — required to run the site, log you in, remember your session and process bookings. These are always active.
  • Analytics — help us understand how people use the site so we can improve it. Only loaded with your consent.
  • Marketing — let advertising platforms measure whether visits or bookings came from an ad. Only loaded with your consent.

What we use

Name / provider Purpose Category Duration
soki_token (localStorage) Keeps you signed in. Necessary Until logout / cleared
soki_lang (localStorage) Remembers your language preference (EN/NL). Necessary Persistent
soki_consent (localStorage) Remembers your cookie choices. Necessary Persistent
Stripe (m, __stripe_mid, __stripe_sid) Fraud prevention during payment. Necessary Up to 1 year
Google Analytics 4 (_ga, _ga_*) Anonymous usage measurement. IP anonymisation on. No advertising signals. Analytics Up to 2 years
Meta Pixel (_fbp, fr) Measure whether you visited or booked after seeing a Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ad. Marketing Up to 90 days
TikTok Pixel (_ttp) Measure whether you visited or booked after seeing a TikTok ad. Marketing Up to 13 months

Analytics and marketing tools are only loaded once you grant consent. If you have not consented, no request is made to Google, Meta or TikTok from this website.

Changing your preferences

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the button below. Your choice is saved locally in your browser.

International data transfer

Google, Meta and TikTok may transfer data to servers outside the European Union (mostly the United States). We rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses, as documented by each provider. If you do not want data to leave the EU, decline the analytics and marketing categories.
